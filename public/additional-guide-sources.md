# Additional Guides — editorial references

Reviewed September 10, 2026. These English and Spanish texts are original pastoral preparation aids, not approved ritual translations, individualized canon-law judgments, or medical advice. Instructors should review local requirements before publishing. Spanish language selection does not imply Spanish national discipline; the Lent guide explicitly identifies U.S. Latin Church practice in both languages.

- Godparents: [Code of Canon Law, canons 872–874](https://press.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann834-878_en.html), [Spanish text](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/esp/documents/cic_libro4_cann872-874_sp.html).
- Sponsors: [canons 892–893](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann879-958_en.html), [USCCB: Christian Initiation of Adults](https://www.usccb.org/beliefs-and-teachings/who-we-teach/christian-initiation-of-adults).
- Confirmation names: [Diocese of Springfield in Illinois preparation guidance](https://dio.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Preparing_for_the_Celebration_of_Confirmation_and_First_Holy_Communion_-_FINAL.pdf). This illustrates local practice, not a universal requirement to select a new name; the guide directs readers to their own parish.
- Easter Vigil: [USCCB: The Roman Missal and the Easter Vigil](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/triduum/roman-missal-and-the-easter-vigil), [Questions on the Sacred Paschal Triduum](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/triduum/questions-and-answers).
- Lent: [USCCB: Fast and Abstinence](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/lent/catholic-information-on-lenten-fast-and-abstinence), [Lent questions and health exceptions](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-worship/liturgical-year/lent), [Pastoral Statement on Penance and Abstinence](https://www.usccb.org/prayer-and-worship/liturgical-year-and-calendar/lent/us-bishops-pastoral-statement-on-penance-and-abstinence), [canons 1249–1253](https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann1244-1253_en.html).
